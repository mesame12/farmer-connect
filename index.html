FCR Calculator

Enter live bird and feed figures for automatic FCR calculation.

FCR = total feed consumed (kg) ÷ total flock weight gained (kg), using 0.042 kg as the Day 0 chick weight.

MPF Feed Planning Guide

For every 100 birds, the normal planning reference is approximately 1 bag Pre Starter + 3 bags Starter + 3 bags Finisher. Finisher may become 4 bags depending on the number of rearing days and actual consumption.
